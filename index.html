5-Day Devotional Series

Living in Community

Based on the sermon by Pastor Bong Baylon — Real Life Christian Communities

Welcome to This Devotional Journey

Five days of prayer, reflection, and community — drawn from the sermon Living in Community.

We were not made to walk with God alone. From the very beginning, God designed us for deep, authentic relationships — with Him and with one another. This five-day devotional invites you to slow down, listen, and respond to what God is saying to you about the community He has placed around you.

Whether you are doing this as part of your personal quiet time or alongside others in a small group, here is how to get the most out of each day:

  • 1
    Find a quiet place. Set aside 15–20 minutes when you will not be interrupted. Begin with a short prayer, asking the Holy Spirit to speak to you through the Word.
  • 2
    Read the Scripture slowly. Let the verse settle in your heart. Read it again if needed. Ask: What is God saying in this passage?
  • 3
    Read the Explanation. This section unpacks the background and meaning of the passage to help deepen your understanding.
  • 4
    Sit with the Reflection question. Be honest with yourself and with God. This is not about the right answer — it is about an open heart.
  • 5
    Write your response in the journal below the question. Writing helps make your thoughts concrete. What did God speak to you today?
  • 6
    Read the Application. Identify one small, practical step you can take today or this week. Commit it to God in prayer.
  • 7
    Use the Discussion question if you are doing this with a group, a friend, or a spiritual director. It is also a great question to bring before God in prayer.
  • 8
    Email your reflection to yourself as a record of your journey, or share it with a trusted friend, mentor, or small group leader for accountability.

💡 Tip: You do not have to rush through all five days at once. Let each day's truth take root before moving to the next. This is a journey, not a race. The Lord is patient, and He will meet you right where you are.

Day 1 of 5
Created for Community
📜
Scripture
▾
"God sets the lonely in families…"
Psalm 68:6 (NIV)
📚
Explanation
▾

Many people today feel lonely and disconnected, even in a world filled with social media and constant communication. We can be surrounded by people and still feel isolated inside.

But loneliness is not part of God's original design for humanity. From the beginning, God created people for relationships and community. In Acts 2, the early believers intentionally shared life together. They worshiped together, prayed together, ate together, and supported one another.

Biblical community is more than being present in the same place. It is about belonging to one another in Christ — learning to love, encourage, and care for one another as spiritual family. Sometimes people withdraw because of hurt, disappointment, fear, or busyness. Yet God often brings healing and growth through authentic relationships with others.

💭
Reflection & Journal
▾

Are there areas in your life where isolation has slowly become normal? What fears or experiences may be making it difficult to connect deeply with others?

Day 2 of 5
More Than Attendance
📜
Scripture
▾
"Every day they continued to meet together in the temple courts…"
Acts 2:46a (NIV)
📚
Explanation
▾

The early church did not limit their relationships to weekly worship gatherings. Their faith was woven into everyday life. They intentionally stayed connected with one another both publicly and privately.

Today, it is possible to attend church regularly while remaining disconnected from people relationally. A person can participate in worship services yet still avoid meaningful fellowship. Technology can help people communicate, but it cannot replace genuine spiritual community.

Real relationships require time, presence, honesty, and shared experiences. The church was never meant to be only a gathering to attend. It was meant to be a spiritual family where believers grow together in faith and love.

💭
Reflection & Journal
▾

Has church become mainly an event to attend rather than a community to belong to? What would it look like to be more fully present and connected?

🌱
Application
▾
Make time this week to engage more intentionally with others in church or in a small group. Stay after the service, start a conversation, or invite someone to connect outside of Sunday worship.
💬
Discussion Question
▾
What usually prevents people from developing deeper relationships within the church?
Day 3 of 5
Eat, Share, and Pray
📜
Scripture
▾
"They broke bread in their homes and ate together with glad and sincere hearts."
Acts 2:46b (NIV)
📚
Explanation
▾

In the early church, sharing meals was not simply about food. Eating together represented acceptance, fellowship, and belonging. Around the table, relationships became deeper and more authentic.

That is why simple practices like eating together, sharing life, and praying together remain powerful today. Genuine community is often built through ordinary moments consistently shared with others.

Authentic relationships require more than occasional meetings. They require time, vulnerability, commitment, and love. People long for places where they can be honest without fear of judgment. Christian community becomes meaningful when believers are willing to be sincere with one another and walk together through both joys and struggles.

💭
Reflection & Journal
▾

How comfortable is it for you to be honest and authentic with others about personal struggles, fears, or needs? What holds you back?

Application
▾

▾

Day 4 of 5
Focused on God and Others
📜
Scripture
▾
"…praising God and enjoying the favor of all the people."
Acts 2:47a (NIV)
📚
Explanation
▾

The early church was deeply connected relationally, but they were not inward-looking. Their focus remained on God and His mission. They worshiped sincerely, and they cared deeply about reaching people with the message of Christ.

Their community life was not centered on comfort or convenience but on the kingdom of God. Churches today can sometimes become too focused on preserving comfort, routines, or traditions. Yet Jesus called His followers to live missionally and to serve others beyond the walls of the church.

A ship may be safest in the harbor, but it was not built to stay there. Healthy Christian communities continue looking outward. They seek opportunities to love people, serve others, and share the gospel faithfully.

💭

Has your spiritual life become too inward-focused or comfort-centered? Where might God be calling you to look outward in love and service?

Day 5 of 5
Becoming the Church
📜
Scripture
▾
"Let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together…"
Hebrews 10:24–25a (NIV)
📚
Explanation
▾

Christian community is not optional for believers. God calls His people to walk together, encourage one another, and grow together spiritually. The early church understood this well — their relationships were marked by consistency, sincerity, worship, and shared purpose.

Today, many people approach church mainly as a place to attend once a week. But the church is not merely a building or a program. The church is a community of people following Jesus together.

Because Jesus lives, believers can experience genuine fellowship, encouragement, healing, and spiritual growth through relationships with one another. The call is not simply to go to church but to become the church through authentic, Christ-centered community.

💭
Reflection & Journal
▾

What kind of contribution are you making to the spiritual community God has placed around you? What is one thing you could do differently to help your community flourish?
